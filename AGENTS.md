# Ture product delivery

Before choosing work, read these current entry points in order:

1. `docs/ture-current-state-ledger.md` — only the **Current delivery board**
   between its BEGIN/END markers selects Now / Next / Blocked work.
2. `docs/ture-master-roadmap.md` — the **Current delivery map**, then the
   acceptance section relevant to the selected outcome.
3. `docs/roadmap-operating-governance.md` — active delivery policy.
4. `docs/open-market-test-contract.md` when preparing an OPEN observation.

Search dated evidence only for the selected dependency; do not read every old
Action or treat an archived "Now", "Next" or approval as current selection.
The repository is the control source. Notion is not a roadmap or sync target.

The product direction is **recommendation intelligence first**: complete fresh
inputs and broad discovery -> contextual ranking -> canonical outcome learning
-> full-charter evaluation -> reversible IF-5 promotion and monitoring. The
longer-term SV-A-SV-U vision remains scientific validation, autonomous internal
paper, IBKR paper and separately approved live/options execution. IBKR is the
future broker; Avanza is maintenance-only. The graduation gate remains binding.

Choose one usable outcome spanning the actual data-to-result chain. Its brief
in the current board names the hypothesis, original population, next missing
link, owner, acceptance, effort limit and decision-changing result. Keep one
primary and at most one selected independent CLOSED slice with isolated work,
stable interfaces and explicit integration order. Budget 4–16 active hours;
initial investigation is capped at four. A second slice is optional and must
not compete for the same data/schema contract. Preserve dirty worktrees and
start from verified current main.

Reuse existing components and integrated proofs. Before OPEN, prove the built
consumer chain with production-equivalent roles/ACLs, configuration and data
contracts at the relevant boundaries; substitute providers only at the edge.
Synthetic success is not market evidence. A reproduced blocker may interrupt
the outcome, but its minimum verified exit must immediately return to that
outcome. Do not extend a closed fix into another helper/control stream.

Freeze experiment population, model, source versions and acceptance before
observing results. Preserve missing/unfavorable outcomes and honest `no_trade`;
provider/data rejection is not an evaluated investment decision. Complete the
frozen comparison or record `evidence_incomplete` with a bounded recovery or
stop. Do not lower thresholds, change old cohorts or force a recommendation.

Update the compact current board in place on material evidence or selection
changes; retain original receipts below it without duplicating whole status
narratives. Maintain the five weekly outcome measures defined in governance.
Separate implemented, environment-verified and product/strategy-accepted states.
PR/test counts are delivery diagnostics, not quality evidence. Review scientific
and production-boundary changes as specified in governance; use existing
authorization and do not invent permission gates. External operations retain
their applicable scope, budgets and release authority.
