## Decision and containment

The `666GS`–`666IS` source-only digest/decoder/receipt chain is
`closed_for_automatic_extension`. Another omitted-key, undefined-value,
descriptor, prototype or receipt variation is rejected: it would add static
detail without closing a named remaining Milestone B capability gate.

Milestone B is `not_complete` and runtime is `closed`. The required disposition
is `redesign_or_stop` for further source-only receipt work. The next product
outcome is not another static receipt claim; it is a single policy-admitted
runtime capability slice that can establish behavior-level idempotency,
owner-bound effects and recovery evidence. That slice must address B-01's
canonical identity/lineage and B-03's transactional handoff alongside new
explicit authority for the B-05 through B-08 prerequisites, followed by the
appropriate B-09 through B-12 evidence. Until then, the allowed result is to
remain blocked by technical evidence.
