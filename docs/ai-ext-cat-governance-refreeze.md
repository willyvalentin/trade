## AI-00: agent intelligence is evidence, not authority

Agent Intelligence may analyze supplied material, investigate through a
separately admitted read-only tool, critique a proposal, and return structured
evidence. Deterministic Ture Core retains all canonical market and trading
truth, risk authority, state authority, execution authorization, broker
integration, and durable audit ownership.

There is no Agent-to-Broker authority path.

The required adoption order is:

1. Start with one bounded `Ture Setup Analyst` capability.
2. Give it only separately admitted read-only tools, first against fixtures or
   offline material and then, only after a new decision, in shadow mode.
3. Preserve privacy-minimized tracing and measured cost, latency and
   reliability metadata without treating telemetry as permission.
4. Evaluate incremental value against a frozen deterministic-Ture baseline and
   realized outcome evidence.
5. Require a separate promotion decision before any effect on ranking,
   recommendation, risk, position, execution eligibility, or another
   canonical product state.
6. Consider multi-agent work only after the one-agent path has measured value,
   bounded failure modes, and a new explicit decision.

An LLM, model output, trace, tool result, local fixture or completed evaluation
does not create a provider call, persistence path, promotion, runtime binding,
or execution authority.

## EXT-00: external capabilities are cost- and evidence-gated

The default acquisition order is:

`local/open-source -> public/free -> sample/sandbox -> free tier -> trial or credits -> month-to-month -> longer commitment after proven product need`

An external capability is a candidate until its own bounded decision records
the problem it solves, the evidence it needs, its cheaper fallback, the cost
state, an exit or downgrade path, and any required operator authorization. A
roadmap item, API key, integration spike, successful test, provider account,
or saved configuration is never spend, runtime, provider, or production
authority.

Potential future capability families remain intentionally uncommitted:

| Family | Default first path | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Durable orchestration | Local TypeScript development and deterministic core controls | A scheduler or orchestration service is not market truth, risk, or execution authority. |
| Data quality and market coverage | Already licensed or public/free sources, then documented evidence gaps | A paid feed needs a separate evidence-backed activation decision. |
| Research lake and replay | Local Parquet plus DuckDB or Polars; independent LEAN replay when useful | Research output is not a canonical recommendation or promotion input by default. |
| Observability and experiment records | Local or free-tier instrumentation first | Telemetry does not authorize data export, model promotion, or runtime binding. |
| Advanced microstructure and options context | Deferred until a concrete product hypothesis requires it | These are non-default spend and need a separate value, licensing and safety review. |

Trials and credits are activated just in time only after an adapter contract,
fixture strategy, and evaluation harness are ready. A trial may be ended or
downgraded when its stated evidence cannot be obtained. No incremental paid
provider commitment is implied by this document.

## CAT-00: Ture-owned WhyMove direction

Ture's planned catalyst direction is a Ture-owned **WhyMove Engine**. It is
not a restoration of Benzinga as a default planned dependency.

Where a future read-only discovery slice is separately authorized and provider
terms permit it, Massive News and Finnhub Company News may be considered as
discovery or evidence leads. They do not become canonical catalyst truth.
Potential leads must be paired with attributable primary evidence where
available, including SEC EDGAR filings, issuer investor-relations or press
releases, FDA material, and official Federal Reserve, BLS, or BEA publications.

The direction has these non-negotiable evidence properties:

- source-aware normalization keeps source identity, capture or effective time,
  provenance, and the distinction between discovery lead and primary evidence;
- snapshot-time boundaries prevent future facts from entering an earlier
  decision context;
- missing, uncertain, stale, disputed, and conflicting evidence are explicit
  states rather than silently repaired confidence;
- a supplied agent summary may describe evidence, but cannot invent or become
  canonical catalyst truth; and
- shadow comparison and ablation against a deterministic baseline precede any
  candidate advisory influence.

The default incremental paid-provider commitment for this direction remains
`$0` until a separate evidence-backed decision changes it. Accounts,
credentials, samples, or a discovery result do not activate calls, trials,
subscriptions, schedulers, persistence, scanning, ranking, recommendations,
risk, execution, deployment, or production behavior.

When this repository decision is verified on exact main, the program-tracking
roadmap must replace its older `Benzinga + SEC catalyst intelligence` shorthand
with this WhyMove direction and label the older wording historical. That mirror
records no provider, spend, runtime, or promotion authority.

## Re-evaluation triggers

A future slice may revisit one of these directions only when it names a
product outcome, an exact bounded authority boundary, the baseline and
evidence it needs, independent verification, and a rollback or containment
condition. In particular, any live source, provider, model, outcome cohort,
promotion, runtime binding, broker interaction, deployment, or production
move needs its own technical and policy admission; none is inherited from this
governance refreeze.

Notion may track the resulting program decision and its synchronization date,
but repository evidence and executable controls remain authoritative for
technical state.
