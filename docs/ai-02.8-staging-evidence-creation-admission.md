# AI-02.8 — Staging evidence-creation admission contract

## Decision

AI-02.8 is the separately selected successor permitted by AI-02.7. It makes
one future staging-only evidence-creation operation precise before anyone
attempts it. The source must be a server-owned completed recommendation and
outcome bundle, not the existing `historical_synthetic` fixture or the private
legacy-preservation relation.

The contract accepts only a frozen plan for one append-only insert into
`public.canonical_evaluation_decisions`. The planned row must have canonical
identity and semantic digest, a complete primary outcome plus diagnostic
horizons, and `inactive_readiness_only=false`. It requires identity-and-digest
idempotency preflight, rollback or removal of a failed proof row, and an
independent minimal readback limited to identity, digest and completion facts.

## Authority boundary

This is a server-only, I/O-free validation primitive. A valid result is
`staging_scope_validated_not_authorized_not_executed`: it does not authorize or
execute a database operation, does not access staging or production, and does
not read a secret or source payload.

It also excludes evaluator, promotion, runtime, provider/model, deployment,
broker and production authority. A future operation remains one separately
authorized staging execution with an independent readback; it cannot use this
contract to form a dataset or invoke evaluation.

## Value and next gate

The contract prevents an unsafe shortcut from reusing the inactive synthetic
receipt or broadening a temporary staging proof into an evaluator or runtime.
It gives the future operator one testable checklist for the only admissible
source of new canonical evidence while keeping all systems fail-closed.

Next gate: `separately_authorized_staging_execution_with_independent_readback`.
