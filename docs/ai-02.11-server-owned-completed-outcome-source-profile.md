## Decision

considered later, without treating it as an available dataset or activating it:

## Exclusions and authority boundary

Caller-supplied snapshots, browser-local storage, fixtures, the
`historical_synthetic` receipt and the legacy-preservation relation are all
explicitly rejected. The profile is server-only and I/O-free. It cannot apply
the v2 migration, read staging or production, read an outcome, write active
evidence, form a dataset, evaluate, promote, bind runtime, contact a provider,
change a deployment, or affect broker or production behavior.

## Value and next gate

default-deny boundary: a later operator has a small, auditable candidate shape
rather than an invitation to import legacy history or fabricate a cohort.
