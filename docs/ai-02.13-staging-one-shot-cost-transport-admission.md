# AI-02.13 — staging one-shot cost and transport admission

## Decision

AI-02.13 bounds the only future source-creation design that can follow the
empty staging outcome-source preflight. The normal scheduled evaluator is not
eligible: it defaults to five batches and ten snapshots. The only candidate is
a temporary deploy-preview adapter limited to one official, server-owned
snapshot, one batch and one provider candle request.

The 15/30/60-minute outcome bundle does not multiply that provider ceiling.
The evaluator builds one reusable candle request per snapshot and filters it
locally into the three horizons. The design therefore requires a separately
authorized maximum of one provider request, rather than assuming a cost from
the number of horizons.

## Authority boundary

The contract is server-only and I/O-free. A valid plan returns
`one_shot_cost_transport_scope_validated_not_authorized_not_executed`; it does
not authorize or execute a branch deployment, staging access, credential or
source-row read, application-owner lookup, evaluator/provider call, outcome
write, v2 migration or active-evidence write.

The next preflight may inspect only non-secret credential-presence and
deployment-context facts needed to decide whether a temporary adapter can be
prepared. It must not retrieve credential values. Dataset formation,
evaluation, promotion, runtime, deployment, broker and production authority
remain excluded.

## Value and next gate

This avoids two unsafe shortcuts: invoking a broad scheduled job to obtain one
sample, and budgeting three provider requests simply because three diagnostic
horizons exist. It makes the small future operation auditable before any
configuration or provider spend is considered.

The next gate is a separately authorized staging-only, non-secret
credential-presence and branch-transport preflight. It may not invoke the
provider or evaluator, deploy the adapter, write an outcome or affect
production behavior.
